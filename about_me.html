A Recent Trip

At the end of May 2026, I took my parents on a trip to Hawaii. This was the first vacation we took together in 6 years. We stayed in Oahu for four days and Maui for three. In Oahu, we hiked Koko Head Trail, shopped in Ala Moana and Wakiki Beach, did the Pearl Harbor tour, and did many of the popular touristy activities. In Maui, we spent the days exploring all the beaches and local site seeing. Although we had a plethora of food options, we spent half of all meals having traditional Chinese food - my parents' comfort food. The one thing we couldn't resist - a classic steak dinner.

My Comfort Food

Growing up in a Chinese household, char siu, a sweet BBQ glazed pork, was a staple protein dish. My family had either bought it from a store or marinated and cooked it at home. The meat is juicy and tender on the outside and when cooked on an open Served with rice, a side of vegetables, and a bowl of soup, this would always be my first choice.
