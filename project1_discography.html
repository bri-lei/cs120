The Sonic Archives
Replicating the Anthems of Alternative Metal & Modern Rock

At a Toxicity Collective show, the sonic chaos doesn't stop at the borders of the System of a Down catalog. While our identity is forged in the hyper-accurate, operatic madness and driving folk-metal rhythms of SOAD's multi-platinum discography, we build a full wall of sound that spans the absolute best of alternative rock's most aggressive era.
From the frantic tempos of Toxicity and the heavy grooves of Mezmerize to the landmark radio rock anthems that dominated the late '90s and early 2000s, our setlist is a calculated dose of pure nostalgia and raw energy. We adapt our performance style to match the precise vibe of your event, seamlessly blending the erratic thrash metal of System with mainstream nu-metal, post-grunge, and hard rock masterpieces that defined a generation. Launch the chaos below and explore the tracks we bring to life.

Song Artist Description
"Chop Suey!" System of a Down, 2001 The definitive anthem featuring frantic vocal duals, heavy drop-tuned staccato riffs, and a massive operatic chorus.
"Toxicity" System of a Down, 2001 A showcase for breakneck time-signature shifts, aggressive drumming, and an explosive, chaotic finale.
"B.Y.O.B." System of a Down, 2005 A blistering thrash-metal assault that abruptly shifts into infectious, heavy funk-infused grooves.
"Aerials" System of a Down, 2001 A dark, hypnotic masterpiece featuring a heavy Middle Eastern-infused bassline and soaring melodic vocals.
"In the End" Linkin Park (2000) The ultimate nu-metal dual-vocal track, perfectly utilizing the contrast between rhythmic delivery and melodic choruses.
"Bodies" Drowning Pool (2001) A high-octane crowd-pleaser built on a driving bass groove and explosive, building vocal aggression.
"Last Resort" Papa Roach (2000) An instantly recognizable, lightning-fast lead guitar riff paired with relentless, high-energy post-grunge energy.
"Killing in the Name" Rage Against the Machine (1992 / Era Staple) An aggressive, politically charged anthem driven by heavy, bouncy basslines and raw vocal power.
"Bring Me to Life" Evanescence (2003) A dramatic, symphonic alt-rock track that highlights powerful lead melodies contrasted with heavy rhythm guitar tracking.
"The Pretender" Foo Fighters (2007) A hard-driving modern rock anthem that transitions seamlessly from a tense, quiet build into an absolute wall of sound.
